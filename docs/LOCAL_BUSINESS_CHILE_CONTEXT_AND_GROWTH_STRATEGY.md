## 1. Founder premise

Palta Local Business must create a durable three-sided benefit:

```text
consumer gets easier / more trustworthy local life
+ owner gets easier / cheaper / more capable business operation
+ Palta earns from deeper value it actually creates
```

The business model must not start by withholding a useful basic presence and then charging to remove artificial pain. The free Business Profile is local infrastructure and acquisition. Revenue follows deeper convenience, automation, transactions, operations and measurable value.

The strategic question is not `how do we sell software to Chilean merchants?`.
It is:

> What real friction do Chilean consumers and small merchants live with today, and what can Palta make materially easier at sustainable cost?

## 2. Chile reality — design for the country that exists

### 2.1 Microbusiness is large, small and often owner-operated

INE reported on 2025-12-10 that Chile had 1,998,178 microentrepreneurs in the VIII Encuesta de Microemprendimiento. 86.8% were self-employed and 54.2% operated informally. Average monthly business profit was CLP 828,612.

Product implications:
- do not design as if every merchant has an office manager, marketing employee or technical administrator;
- the owner may be the cashier, buyer, marketer and service worker at the same time;
- setup and daily maintenance must be extremely small;
- useful free/low-cost tools can matter materially;
- formal/fiscal capabilities must be progressive and lawful rather than used as a registration wall for every discoverable business presence.

Source: Instituto Nacional de Estadísticas, `En Chile hay cerca de 2 millones de personas microemprendedoras`, 2025-12-10.
https://www.ine.gob.cl/sala-de-prensa/prensa/general/noticia/2025/12/10/en-chile-hay-cerca-de-2-millones-de-personas-microemprendedoras

### 2.2 Social presence is common; integrated digital selling is much less common

The Cámara de Comercio de Santiago reported in 2025 that more than 70% of Chilean Pymes were present on social networks, while about 25% had a digital sales channel.

Product implication:

> Palta should not begin by asking a merchant to abandon Instagram, WhatsApp, Google or an existing marketplace. It should turn fragmented presence into one coherent Business identity and progressively reduce repeated work.

Source: Cámara de Comercio de Santiago, `El comercio digital impulsa el crecimiento de los emprendedores en Chile`, 2025-07-10.
https://www.ccs.cl/2025/07/10/el-comercio-digital-impulsa-el-crecimiento-de-los-emprendedores-en-chile/

### 2.3 Government support confirms the same capability gap

Sercotec's 2026 Ruta Digital Kit Digital RM explicitly funds websites, digital accounting/CRM, ERP for inventory/electronic invoicing, marketing and web positioning. This is useful evidence that these are not invented SaaS categories: they are recognized capability gaps for Chilean micro and small businesses.

Product implications:
- Palta owner guidance may legitimately cover discoverability, content, sales, customer management, stock, fiscal preparation and digital operations;
- guidance should be short and actionable, not a lecture library;
- Palta should automate clerical work where safe and route professional judgment to the appropriate accountant/legal/provider role.

Source: Sercotec, `Ruta Digital Kit Digital Región Metropolitana de Santiago 2026`.
https://www.sercotec.cl/convocatoria/ruta-digital-kit-digital-region-metropolitana-de-santiago-2026/

## 3. What to learn from Korea

### 3.1 Danggeun — free local presence becomes relationship infrastructure

Danggeun Business Profile reports more than 1.7 million Business Profiles and more than 9.8 million regular-customer relationships as of January 2026. Its Business Profile combines local presence with news/posts, chat, coupons, regular-customer management and specialist actions such as quote, booking and pickup.

Palta lesson:
- the free page must be useful enough to claim and maintain;
- the business itself becomes neighborhood content;
- coupon/post/chat/follow are not isolated widgets — they turn discovery into an ongoing local relationship;
- specialist transaction capabilities attach after the common profile rather than replacing it.

Sources:
https://business.daangn.com/business-profile/about
https://business.daangn.com/edu/vod/a1a1915f-7444-4310-89d0-aedb85741e85/

### 3.2 Kakao — owner-maintained facts should propagate to discovery surfaces

Kakao Business store management lets owners manage location, hours, prices, website, tags, news, coupons and review replies for information exposed in Kakao Map.

Palta lesson:

> One owner-maintained truth should feed every Palta projection that needs it.

The owner must not re-enter hours for map, web page, search and order separately.

Source: Kakao Business guide, `매장관리`.
https://kakaobusiness.gitbook.io/main/channel/run/mystore

## 4. What to learn from the United States

### 4.1 Nextdoor — free business page + local posting + paid reach

Nextdoor offers a free Business Page, current contact information, recommendations and unlimited free Business Posts. Paid Local Deals/ads extend reach beyond the free neighborhood relationship.

Palta lesson:
- useful free local presence and posting can coexist with paid reach;
- paid distribution should be visibly separate from organic relevance;
- local radius/neighborhood is a natural commercial unit, but Palta must use its own distance/life-area model rather than copy another platform's geography.

Sources:
https://business.nextdoor.com/en-us/getting-started/business-page
https://business.nextdoor.com/en-us/getting-started/business-post

### 4.2 Square — operations generate useful customer context

Square Customer Directory is a free CRM integrated with transactions and can show purchase history, loyalty status and multi-channel activity. Square then sells deeper marketing/loyalty/automation capabilities.

Palta lesson:
- customer context is most useful when it arises from legitimate business interaction rather than manual database-building;
- advanced automation is a natural paid layer;
- Palta should keep stricter purpose/consent separation than simply assuming a transaction authorizes marketing.

Sources:
https://squareup.com/us/en/point-of-sale/features/customer-directory
https://squareup.com/us/en/marketing

## 5. Palta differentiation

Palta should not become a Chilean copy of Danggeun, Nextdoor, Square, Google Maps or Uber Eats.

Its differentiator is the combination:

```text
living local graph
+ current operating truth
+ personal/local context
+ free common Business page
+ voluntary customer relationship
+ owner Business Partner Home
+ open external-channel integration
+ Chile-specific admin/fiscal help
+ later AI assistance
```

For a consumer:

```text
Where am I?
What can I actually do / eat / buy / solve here now?
Which place is real and usable?
What is worth knowing even if I did not search for it?
```

For an owner:

```text
What needs attention now?
Is my public information still true?
How can customers understand and find me more easily?
What repetitive work can Palta remove?
What obligation or customer follow-up should I not forget?
```

## 6. Search acquisition — earn Google traffic rather than trying to game Google

The goal is not to scrape Google search results or manufacture keyword pages. The goal is to publish genuinely useful Palta local information that Google and other search engines can crawl and users are happy to land on.

Google's current guidance says local ranking is mainly based on relevance, distance and prominence, and there is no way to pay Google for better organic local ranking. Complete, accurate business information helps relevance. Google also says it can use publicly crawlable business websites as one source for Business Profile/local information.

Canonical Palta model:

```text
Google / other web search
  -> useful public Palta page
  -> user gets the answer immediately
  -> optional deeper Palta action
  -> save/follow/order/quote/booking when useful
  -> repeat relationship
```

Never put an app-install wall in front of the useful public answer.

Sources:
https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091
https://support.google.com/business/answer/2721884

## 7. One Business = one canonical public page

Recommended canonical form:

```text
https://somospalta.cl/negocios/<stable-business-slug>
```

Rules:
- one real Business location/identity gets one canonical public page according to the canonical identity/branch model;
- do not clone the same page as `/vitacura/x`, `/las-condes/x`, `/providencia/x` simply to target city/comuna keywords;
- use `rel=canonical`, canonical sitemap URLs and redirects consistently when URL migration occurs;
- duplicate/unresolved records should be `noindex` until identity is resolved;
- permanently closed records need a deliberate archival/search policy; do not silently present them as active.

Source: Google Search Central canonicalization guidance.
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls

## 8. Structured data and public HTML

The public renderer should expose useful human-readable HTML and applicable `LocalBusiness` structured data from the same canonical Business truth.

Possible projection:
- canonical URL;
- business name;
- most specific applicable LocalBusiness subtype(s);
- description;
- address/service area as appropriate;
- geo coordinates for public fixed locations;
- phone/contact;
- images;
- opening-hours specifications;
- existing website/social `sameAs` links;
- current/seasonal operating information visibly in the page;
- real services/menu/catalog where owner/publication policy permits;
- posts/coupons where current and applicable.

Google recommends the most specific applicable `LocalBusiness` subtype and recommends sitemap submission to help future discovery.

Source:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business

## 9. Local/category landing pages — only when they are genuinely useful

Pages such as these can be valuable:

```text
Restaurantes en Algarrobo
Cafés abiertos hoy en Vitacura
Gasfíter que atienden en Las Condes
Panaderías cerca de Ñuñoa
```

But they must be real discovery products, not SEO wrappers.

An indexable local/category page should contain materially useful, distinct information such as:
- actual current businesses serving the area;
- distance/area relevance;
- real current operating state where confidently known;
- service/category distinctions;
- useful filters/actions;
- crawlable links to canonical Business pages;
- enough inventory/value to justify an independent page.

Do **not** generate every `category × comuna × neighborhood × keyword` combination automatically. Google explicitly prohibits doorway abuse such as many city/region pages that simply funnel users to the same destination, and scaled low-value content can also violate spam policies.

Source:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies

## 10. Search-demand feedback loop

Palta should learn from search demand without turning analytics into surveillance.

Safe aggregate loop:

```text
public/internal query demand
 -> aggregate query/service/locality patterns
 -> identify missing taxonomy aliases or missing useful pages
 -> improve Business/service structured data
 -> improve useful public/local discovery
 -> measure aggregate landing/action outcomes
```

Examples:
- many users search `vulcanización`, but businesses use only `neumáticos` -> suggest that verified service alias to relevant owners;
- many searches in Algarrobo ask for `abierto ahora`, but operating evidence is stale -> prioritize owner reconfirmation rather than fabricate open status;
- users find a Business page but repeatedly leave because prices/menu/contact are missing -> suggest that specific free improvement to the owner.

Raw identifiable search history is not automatically permanent personalization memory.

## 11. Business PR assistance — free presence, paid repeated-work removal

The free Business Profile should help an owner communicate clearly even without a marketer, without requiring OAuth or third-party API authorization.

Free baseline:
- QR to canonical Business page;
- printable counter/storefront/opening-hours card from Business data;
- simple Palta share card/image template;
- basic Palta post/news and verified-owner coupon;
- profile-completeness/freshness guidance;
- photo/service/price presentation tips;
- public Instagram/Facebook/TikTok/Google/WhatsApp/website links;
- one-tap outbound navigation to those public channels where applicable.

**FREE external-channel boundary: `LINK_ONLY`.**

A merchant can paste a public Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Google, WhatsApp, website or marketplace URL and expose it from the Palta Business Profile. This must not require the merchant to convert an account type, authorize OAuth, give Palta an API token, or buy a plan.

Paid/entitled value begins when Palta materially performs work across external channels:
- assisted-share workflows that Palta prepares/manages beyond a plain public link;
- publish once to supported authorized channels;
- read/sync supported channel data or metrics;
- schedule/adapt content;
- consolidate metrics;
- customer segmentation under consent;
- repeat-service/win-back automation;
- orders/POS/inventory/channel reconciliation;
- team workflows;
- advanced fiscal/admin preparation.

The owner should not be shown an automation upsell merely because links exist. A paid suggestion should be grounded in real friction such as repeated cross-channel publishing or another observed repetitive task.

## 12. External channel doctrine

Each external channel should be represented as an adapter/connection to the canonical Business, not as a duplicate business system.

Connection levels:

```text
LINK_ONLY          = free public URL / outbound navigation
ASSISTED_SHARE     = entitlement required
CONNECTED_READ     = entitlement + provider authorization/capability required
CONNECTED_PUBLISH  = entitlement + provider authorization/capability required
CONNECTED_OPERATE  = entitlement + provider authorization/capability required
```

Important implications:
- a personal Instagram account can still be a useful public link even when API publishing is unavailable;
- a public URL never proves OAuth/API authorization;
- a technically authorized external account never proves commercial entitlement;
- automated publishing requires both explicit provider capability/authorization **and** an active Palta entitlement;
- owner authorization/consent is mandatory before managing a Google Business Profile or other third-party account;
- secrets/tokens remain server-side in the integration layer, not public Business data;
- external marketplaces remain sources/channels, not canonical Business/Product/Customer truth;
- plan/pricing decisions belong to Shared Entitlement/Pricing policy, not Local Business code.

### 12.1 Downgrade / cancellation rule

If a merchant loses or cancels the entitlement for an external-channel integration:

```text
CONNECTED_PUBLISH / ASSISTED_SHARE / CONNECTED_*
        -> automation/integration stops
        -> public URL remains
        -> effective free mode becomes LINK_ONLY
```

Do not punish plan cancellation by deleting the merchant's Instagram/Facebook/TikTok/Google/website link from the free Business Profile. OAuth/token retention or revocation is a separate security/integration-lifecycle decision; public link presence is not.

This protects the free profile's usefulness while keeping the paid value proposition honest: Palta charges for doing work, not for showing a URL.

## 13. Reviews and reputation

Palta should eventually help legitimate customers leave reviews and help owners respond, but never manufacture reputation.

Rules:
- never buy or incentivize positive Google reviews in violation of platform policy;
- no review gating (only asking happy customers while suppressing unhappy ones);
- a neutral `review us` QR/link after a legitimate interaction may be useful where provider policy allows;
- Palta's own review system, if built, requires verified-use signals, reporting/moderation and anti-fraud rules before ratings become authoritative.

## 14. Owner acquisition / outreach

The strongest owner pitch is not a feature catalogue.

Preferred sequence:

```text
show the owner the existing useful Palta presence / local demand
 -> help claim/correct it
 -> give an immediately useful free improvement
 -> keep information current with minimal work
 -> demonstrate customer discovery/relationship value
 -> suggest deeper tool only when the business has the matching friction
```

Possible first-contact value:
- `Your business is already discoverable here; confirm these facts.`
- `Here is your free QR.`
- `Your winter hours appear uncertain; confirm with one tap.`
- `Customers search this service term; do you actually offer it?`
- `Add your Instagram/WhatsApp link so people can reach you.`

The relationship should feel like help first, not a disguised sales call.

## 15. Technical order from this research

Current implementation direction:

```text
1. living Business operational truth
2. canonical free profile
3. canonical public-web/SEO projection
4. free LINK_ONLY external-channel projection
5. owner next-best-action policy
6. operator AI-readable messaging bridge contract
7. actual public web renderer + sitemap when web runtime is chosen
8. save/follow/coupon/customer relationship implementation
9. aggregate local/category pages only after real inventory/data quality exists
10. Search Console / aggregate search-demand feedback after public web launch
11. entitled external-channel adapters only when real owner friction + provider/API readiness are proven
```

Do not create a second Business identity, second messaging engine, second map or second independently maintained business website while implementing this sequence.

## 16. Cost doctrine

The work in this document should start with low fixed cost:
- organic Google indexing has no indexing fee;
- public external-channel links require no OAuth/API infrastructure and should remain the free default;
- canonical/static/edge-rendered pages should reuse existing Business data rather than require AI per page view;
- JSON-LD, sitemap generation, QR and templated assets are deterministic and should not require generative AI per request;
- AI should be reserved for ambiguous classification, translation/operator assistance, content help or higher-value automation rather than every read;
- third-party APIs/providers are attached only when value and pricing justify them;
- a paid external-channel integration should be evaluated on owner time saved and provider/API cost before activation.

Any future paid provider must be benchmarked before activation. Current Local Business contracts do not require a new paid provider.
